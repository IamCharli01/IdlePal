# 🧭 Expeditions, the room and rubber-ducking

Three things for the days you're heads-down.

## Expeditions

Send your pet to **The Legacy Monolith** (30 min), **The Cloud** (60 min) or **The Kernel** (2 h). While it's away its needs are frozen, so you can stay in flow. It comes back with coins, a food and, sometimes, one of **12 artefacts** for your Collection (the Golden Rice Ball only exists out there). Trail Mix from the shop makes the next trip 25% shorter. You can recall it early, but it comes home empty-handed.

## The room

The **Room** section of the shop sells fifteen decorations that live on the LCD behind your pet: a monstera, a server rack that blinks, a coffee machine, a beanbag, an aquarium, posters, a window, an arcade cabinet… One per spot. Your pet wanders over to use the floor pieces.

## Rubber-duck a bug

Stuck? Press **Talk** and explain the bug to your pet in writing. It nudges you with the questions a good pair would ask (*"When did it last work?"*, *"What changed since then?"*), and pays a few coins when you say it helped. Nothing you type leaves the panel.

## The diary

Your pet keeps a **Diary** tab: hatchings, evolutions, first tastes, personal bests, expeditions, and a line each morning about what you coded the day before.
