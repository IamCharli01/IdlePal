# ⚙️ Make it yours

- **🏖️ Vacation mode**: freeze time completely, for holidays or crunch weeks.
- **Speed**: set `idlepal.speed` to `0.5` for a lower-maintenance pet.
- **Notifications**: `all`, `important`, or `off`.
- **Break reminders**: a nudge after 90 minutes of continuous coding (configurable).
- **Sync**: your pet follows you to other machines via Settings Sync.
- **Share**: save a PNG card of your pet from the Stats tab.

Everything stays on your machine (or in your own Settings Sync). No telemetry, no accounts.
