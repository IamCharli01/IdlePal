# 🍙 Keep it happy

| Need | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 🍙 Hungry | **Feed** a meal. Rice balls are always free, and the drawer suggests what fits best |
| 💔 Bored | **Play** a mini-game, or just keep coding |
| 💩 Messy | **Clean** up. Mess makes it sick |
| 💊 Sick | Give **medicine** |
| 💤 Tired | Turn the **lights** off so it can sleep |

**Care shapes evolution.** Every time a need is neglected (starving, miserable, filthy, or sick for 30 minutes) it counts as a *care mistake*. Few mistakes give rarer forms; many give… a Gremlin.

While VS Code is closed, time runs at ¼ speed. Your pet can't get sick or die while you're away.

## Food tips

- **Every pet has a favourite, and a food it hates.** Feed a variety of things to find out which.
- **Mix it up.** The same food three times running loses its punch. Eat from all four groups (Meals, Snacks, Drinks, Treats) in a day for a **Balanced Diet** bonus.
- **Mind the caffeine.** Coffee and Energy Drinks crash 45 minutes later. Green Tea doesn't.
- **Feeling poorly?** Chicken Soup sometimes cures a cold.
- **Check the Chef's Special:** one food a day is 25% off.
