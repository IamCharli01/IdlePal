# 🎾 Play mini-games

Fourteen games, all on the LCD. Each game costs a little energy, cheers your pet up and pays coins. Your personal best is saved for every game, and one game a week is the **Weekly Challenge**: double coins, and your best score goes on your team card.

**Arcade**

| Game | How to play |
| --- | --- |
| **Bug Squash** 🐛 | Bugs pop up for 20 seconds. Click them, or press **1–9** (numpad layout). |
| **Deploy Dash** 🚀 | Run to production. **Space** or tap to jump the bugs, which speed up over time. |
| **Stack Overflow** 🧱 | Drop each sliding layer with **Space**. Any overhang gets sliced off. |
| **Linked List** 🐍 | Snake. Steer with the **arrow keys** and append nodes without dereferencing yourself. |
| **Pipeline** 🐦 | Flap through the CI gates with **Space**. Miss one and the build fails. |
| **Tech Debt** 🧹 | Breakout. **← →** move the paddle, **Space** launches. Three balls to clear the wall. |
| **Packet Pong** 🏓 | Rally against your pet. **↑ ↓** or tap the top and bottom halves of the screen. |

**Brain**

| Game | How to play |
| --- | --- |
| **Left or Right** ↔️ | Guess which way your pet will look, 5 rounds. Arrow keys. |
| **Rubber Duck Says** 🦆 | Watch the duck flash arrows, then repeat the sequence. It grows every round. |
| **Hot Keys** ⌨️ | Type code tokens (`=>`, `!==`, `git push`…) as fast as you can in 30 seconds. |
| **Merge Conflict** 🔀 | Two versions of a line. Keep the one without the bug (**←** ours, **→** theirs). |
| **Git Bisect** 🔍 | Commit 1 is good, commit N is bad. Find the first bad one in as few tests as a binary search. |
| **Regex Racer** 🔤 | A pattern and four strings. Pick the one it matches (**1–4**). 8 rounds, 60 seconds. |
| **Big-O** 📈 | Read the snippet, call its complexity (**1–4**). 6 rounds. |

Winning lots of games helps your pet grow into a **Starlet**. Press **Esc** to quit any game.
