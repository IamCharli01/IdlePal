# 🥚 Hatch your egg

Your pet lives in the **Explorer**, underneath your file tree. Look for the **IdlePal** section at the bottom. Drag its divider to resize it, or drag the section anywhere you like.

Prefer a whole sidebar tab? Set `idlepal.location` to `activityBar`.

- The egg hatches after **about a minute** with VS Code open.
- Your pet only ages while VS Code is open, so it grows up alongside you.
- The status bar (bottom right) always shows its mood. Hover it for stats and quick actions.

| Stage | Reached after |
| --- | --- |
| Baby | 1 minute |
| Child | 1 hour |
| Teen | 6 hours |
| Adult | 24 hours |
