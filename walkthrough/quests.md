# 📜 Daily quests and focus

**Quests.** Every day you get three fresh quests, such as *type 1,500 characters*, *make a commit* or *finish a focus session*. Each pays coins, and finishing all three pays a **+20 bonus**. They're the same in every window and never re-roll mid-day.

**Focus sessions.** Press **Start** in the pet panel, or `Ctrl+Alt+T` → *Start focus session*.

- 25 minutes of work (configurable), then a 5-minute break.
- Your pet sits with you, and notifications go quiet.
- Finishing earns coins and a big happiness boost. Stopping early earns nothing.

The countdown shows in the status bar. Click it to stop.
