# 🤝 Bring your team

Team pets need **no server and no accounts**. They travel with git.

1. Run **IdlePal: Share My Pet with This Repo**.
2. A small card appears in `.idlepal/<your-name>.json`.
3. Commit it. When teammates pull, your pet shows up in their **Team** tab, and theirs in yours.

**What's shared:** your display name, your pet's name, species, mood and outfit, your streak and your achievement count.

**What's never shared:** coding time, characters, commits or anything about your files. This is a habitat, not a timesheet.

Toggle it off at any time and your card is removed. Prefer to keep it out of the repo? Add `.idlepal/` to `.gitignore`.
