# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of **IdlePal**, a pixel-art pet that lives in VS Code and grows with your coding.

**Your pet**
- Egg → baby → child → teen → adult. It only ages while VS Code is open, and time away runs at a quarter speed with a health floor, so it can't die while you're gone.
- **13 species with branching evolution.** Care decides the child and teen forms; how you code decides the adult: Starlet (games), Octocoder (lots of coding), Dataray (three or more languages), Nocturne (late-night coding), Phoenix (a near-perfect third generation), Mochimon, Blobbo or, with neglect, Gremlin.
- **9 personality traits** that change how it behaves, and a 1-in-16 chance of hatching **shiny**.
- **24 foods** in four groups, each with its own pixel art. Every pet has a secret favourite and a food it hates; variety, a Balanced Diet bonus, caffeine crashes and a daily Chef's Special keep feeding interesting. One food only exists on expeditions.
- Vacation mode freezes time. A memorial remembers pets that have passed on.

**14 mini-games**
- Arcade: Bug Squash, Deploy Dash, Stack Overflow, Linked List (snake), Pipeline (flap through CI gates), Tech Debt (breakout) and Packet Pong (a rally against your pet).
- Brain: Left or Right, Rubber Duck Says, Hot Keys, Merge Conflict, Git Bisect, Regex Racer and Big-O.
- Personal bests for every game, and every game is proven winnable by an autopilot in CI.
- **Weekly Challenge:** one game a week pays double coins, the same for everyone.

**Things to do while you code**
- **Rubber Duck mode:** explain a bug to your pet in writing. It nudges you with the questions a good pair would ask, and pays a few coins when you say it helped. The text never leaves the panel.
- **Expeditions:** send your pet to the Legacy Monolith, the Cloud or the Kernel for 30 minutes to 2 hours. Its needs freeze while it's away, and it comes back with coins, food and sometimes one of 12 artefacts.
- **Focus sessions:** a Pomodoro timer your pet does with you. Notifications go quiet and you're paid when you finish.
- **Daily quests:** three a day, with a bonus for clearing all three.
- Break reminders after long sessions.

**Coins for real work**
- Typing, saving, git commits, clearing every error, passing tasks, saving test files and daily check-ins all earn coins, capped each day. Streaks, with a Streak Shield to forgive a missed day.

**The shop**
- Mx. Stash, a shopkeeper you can haggle with. Daily deals, a discount for today's commits, Mystery Boxes with box-only exclusives, bundles, sell-backs and a savings goal.
- A wardrobe of 18 hats, 6 face items and 6 held props, 15 screen palettes, consumables (Streak Shield, Quest Reroll, Focus Booster, Vitamins, Lucky Charm, Trail Mix), and every cosmetic previewed on your own pet.
- **The Room:** 15 decorations drawn on the LCD behind your pet, which it walks over and uses.
- **The Vault:** six secret transformations, each with its own animated world, unlocked only by promo codes released as drops.

**Collection and stats**
- 55 tiered achievement tracks (184 tiers), a species collection, artefacts and a diary your pet writes itself.
- Coding time today and over the last 7 days, per-language time, a weekly recap, CSV export and share cards.

**Team pets, no backend**
- Opt in and a small card about your pet (never your coding time) goes into `.idlepal/` in the repo. Teammates who commit theirs appear in the Team tab, with a Weekly Challenge leaderboard.

**Fair and private**
- No telemetry, no accounts, no network. Your save lives in VS Code's storage and, if you choose, Settings Sync. Export and import your save at any time.
- Strict webview Content Security Policy, and every message from the webview, team card and imported save is validated.
